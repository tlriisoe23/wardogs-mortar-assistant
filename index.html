WARDOGS // QUICK-START FIELD MANUAL & HOW-TO GUIDE

Start-to-Finish Voice Workflow · Tactical Map · 3D DEM Terrain · Spotter Nudge & Clipboard · Screen OCR

🎙️ 1. Start-to-Finish Voice Fire Mission (Zero Alt-Tab)

  • Phase 0 — Pre-Game Setup: Allow microphone access in Chrome/Edge, then toggle ON 🎮 In-Game Global PTT (No Alt-Tab) in the left panel. Default key is Mouse 4 (Side Back) (or click ⌨️ Bind Key to bind any mouse/keyboard key). Hold your key inside WARDOGS — a high radio chirp confirms the mic is live.
  • Phase 1 — Choose Map & Weapon: Speak "Bakurani", "Ozeti", "Zestafona", or "Firing range". Switch weapons anytime by saying "Switch to mortar" (L81 132–684m), "Switch to SPH" (SPH-2 High Arc 780–2629m), or "Low arc" (SPH-2 Flat 1181–2629m).
  • Phase 2 — Set Your Gun Position: Open your in-game map (M), check your coordinates, and say "Mortar 78 point 5 by 69 point 5" or 4-digit grid style "Mortar 78 50 by 69 50". Say "Lock mortar" so you don't accidentally move your gun.
  • Phase 3 — Call Target & Fire: Say "Target 82 point 18 by 71 point 53", "Target 82 18 by 71 53", or a landmark name like "Target Tower 1" / "Target Sunflower Church". The AI officer immediately speaks back Bearing (°), Range (m), and Elevation (MIL). Say "Shot" when firing to start the Time-of-Flight splash countdown timer.
  • Phase 4 — Walk In Missed Shells:
    • By Splash Coordinates: Say "Strike 82.50 by 71.80" or "Hit 82 50 by 71 80" — automatically computes the miss vector and shifts your gun aim onto the target.
    • By Relative Miss: Say "Missed long 40 left 20" (auto-inverts to Drop 40m, Right 20m).
    • By Direct FO Shift: Say "Add 50", "Drop 25", "Left 20", or "Right 15".

🗺️ 2. Tactical Map & Classic Fire Control

  • Left-Click Map: Places 🔴 Enemy Target and speaks the new firing solution.
  • Right-Click Map: Places 🔵 Gun / Mortar (disabled when 🔒 Lock Gun is active).
  • Shift + Left-Click Map: Marks 💥 Shell Impact / Splash where your round landed and automatically applies the aim offset to hit the red target on your next shot.
  • Zoom & Pan: Scroll wheel to zoom (up to 32×), middle-click or Space+Drag to pan, or click 🔍 Fit Mission.
  • 🌐 Fire Control Tab: Embeds wardog-fire-control locally with 8.5× deep zoom and 2-way live sync (voice & main app changes update the Fire Control pins automatically).

⛰️ 3. Terrain Height (Auto DEM), 3D Recon & SPH-2 Tilt

  • Auto DEM: ON: Automatically samples 20m-interval elevation maps for Bakurani, Ozeti, and Zestafona to compute ΔZ = Target Z − Gun Z and applies RK4 ballistic height compensation.
  • Ridge Clearance & 3D FPV: Checks every point along your line of fire for hill collisions (✅ Trajectory Clear vs. ⚠️ CREST BLOCKED) and renders a 3D terrain wire-mesh + LOF PROFILE curve below the map.
  • 🚜 SPH-2 Hull (°): The SPH-2 gun has a built-in 5.0° hull tilt. Enter your vehicle compass heading (or say "Hull heading 180") when firing sideways/behind the hull, and align the 2 side tilt markers below STABILIZED / ASL on flat ground.

🎯 4. Spotter Nudge Pad & Clipboard Sync

  • ▲ ADD / ▼ DROP / ◀ LEFT / ▶ RIGHT: Shifts your gun aim relative to your Gun-to-Target Line of Fire by the selected STEP (10m, 25m, 50m, or 100m).
  • Preserves True Target: Nudge adjustments build a separate 🎯 GUN AIM offset banner so your original Target coordinates are never lost. Click Zero Offset to reset.
  • 📋 Paste Tgt / 💥 Paste Hit: Copy any coordinate text from Discord or squad chat (Ctrl+C) and click Paste Tgt (sets Target) or Paste Hit (auto-corrects miss).
  • 📤 Copy Sol: Copies a formatted 1-line firing solution to your clipboard to paste into chat.

💬 5. Private Coord Chat (CL Rule) & Smart OCR

  • The CL Rule (Current Location vs. Target): Add CL to a coordinate line (e.g. 78.5 69.5 CL or compact 7850 6950 CL) to set & lock your Gun / Mortar position. Every coordinate line after that without CL (e.g. 82.18 71.53 or 8218 7153) is automatically assumed to be the Target until you enter a new CL line!
  • Add HIT / SPLASH for Corrections: Typing or scanning 82.50 71.80 HIT automatically calculates the miss vector and shifts your gun aim onto the target.
  • 3 Ways to Feed Lines (Chat Box, Screen OCR, or In-Game Ctrl+C): Type directly into 💬 Private Coord Chat, or turn on 📷 Auto-OCR to read in-game chat lines off your screen (or press Ctrl+A → Ctrl+C inside the in-game chat box for 100% instant capture without even sending the chat message!).
